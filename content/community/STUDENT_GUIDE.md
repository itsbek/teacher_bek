# Want to publish your writing? Here's how 👋

Hey! So you want to get your writing up on the community wall — that's awesome. Here's everything you need to know. It's way simpler than it sounds.

---

## What should I write about?

Honestly, anything. Here are some ideas if you're stuck:

- Something that happened to you this week
- A place in your city you love (or hate)
- Your opinion on food, music, travel, anything
- A childhood memory
- Something you learned recently that surprised you
- A mini-story — real or made up
- A person who has influenced you

There's no right or wrong topic. Your voice and your experience are the whole point.

---

## The only rules (just three, promise)

1. **Write in English** — that's kind of the whole deal 😄
2. **Keep it under 200 words** — short and punchy beats long and rambly every time
3. **Make it yours** — no copy-pasting from the internet, please

---

## How long is 200 words?

It's roughly half a page. Sounds short, but it's actually the perfect length — enough space to say something real, not so much that you stress about it.

Not sure if you're under the limit? Paste your text into **wordcounter.net** — takes two seconds.

---

## How to submit

1. Head to the community page on the website
2. Scroll down to the **Student Writing Wall** section
3. Fill in the form — your name, level, title, and your piece
4. Hit submit!

That's it. Teacher Bek will review it, usually within a day or two.

---

## What happens after you submit

Your writing goes to Bek for review before anything goes public. If there are small typos to fix, Bek might tidy them up — but the words stay yours, always.

Once it's approved, it goes live on the wall with your **first name and level only**. No last names, no contact info, nothing else.

---

## Questions?

Ask in class or message on Zalo. You can also put a question in the title field when you submit — Bek will see it.

Go on, be the first one to post! 🙌
