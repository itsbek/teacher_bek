## Opening hook

Start with a concrete observation, a surprising fact, or a short story that immediately
shows why this post matters. Don't open with "In this article I will explain..."

## First main point

Explain the first idea clearly. Use specifics — names of techniques, numbers, real
examples from the classroom.

### A subsection if needed

Break long sections into smaller chunks. Each subsection should have one clear takeaway.

## Second main point

Keep the reader moving forward. Each section should answer a question the previous
section raised.

> A pull quote or key takeaway that stands out visually.

## Practical takeaway

End with something the reader can do today. Specific and actionable beats vague advice.
