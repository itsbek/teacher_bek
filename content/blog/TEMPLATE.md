## Opening hook

Start with a concrete observation, a surprising fact, or a short story.
Don't open with "In this article I will explain..."

## First main point

Explain the idea clearly. Use specifics — names, numbers, real classroom examples.

## Second main point

Keep the reader moving. Each section should answer a question the previous one raised.

> A pull quote or key takeaway.

## Practical takeaway

End with something the reader can do today. Specific beats vague.
