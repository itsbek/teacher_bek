## What to do first

1. **Fill in the answer form (P1.1).** It unlocks copy, pricing and the claims we can make. Send it back as soon as you can. Leave anything you do not know blank.
2. **Send the consent form (P1.4 consent) to a Vietnamese lawyer** for review, PDPL Law 91/2025/QH15. Do this early, because the shoot and testimonials cannot start until the form is approved.
3. **Start the testimonial messages (P1.8).** Check Q7 first: only real reviews with agreement may be shown. Send the Zalo script to parents you know well.
4. **Write the handwriting marks (P1.6).** You can do this at home in one sitting. Use the same pen for all 60 marks.
5. **Book the shoot (P1.4 shot plan)** once the consent form is approved and you have signed forms from the families taking part. Pick a real lesson day and check the sunset time.

## Rules for all files

- Do not fill in prices, credentials, numbers or claims unless you can prove them.
- Keep signed consent forms in a private folder. Do not share them with the developer by public link.
- Never publish a child's name, face or school without a signed form.
