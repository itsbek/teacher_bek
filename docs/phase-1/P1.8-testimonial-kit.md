# P1.8 — Testimonial kit (Zalo script, questions, video guide, tracking)

Use this kit to ask parents for a short testimonial. Only ask parents whose child is still studying or has recently finished, and who you know will be happy to be named. Check Q7 in the owner answer form first: the 5 current testimonials must be verified as real and consented before they are used.

**Always get the signed consent form (P1.4) before any testimonial is published.**

## 4. How to record a 20–30 second phone video

Ask the parent to follow these steps. Send them this list if helpful.

1. **Hold the phone vertical** (portrait, 9:16). Do not turn it sideways.
2. **Light:** stand facing a window, so the light falls on your face. Do not stand with the window behind you.
3. **Quiet room:** turn off the TV and fans if possible. Close the door.
4. **Phone position:** at eye level, about 1 metre away. Lean the phone on a stack of books or a cup if there is no tripod. Keep it still.
5. **Background:** plain wall, no mess. Avoid showing a child's school name, home address or room number.
6. **Sound:** speak clearly, about 30 cm from the phone. Put the phone on silent (do not let calls or notifications interrupt).
7. **Length:** 20–30 seconds. One take is fine. Say the child's first name only if you want.
8. **No filters or effects.** Send the video as it is (do not compress it or send it as a sticker).
9. **Permission:** the video will be sent back to the parent to approve before it is used.

## 5. Consent reminder

Before any testimonial is published:

- Send the consent form (P1.4) to the parent: [link].
- Make sure it is **signed**. Keep the signed copy in the private folder, not in the website folder.
- If the parent only wants their first name or initial shown, follow that choice exactly.
- If a parent withdraws, remove the testimonial from the site within the time written on the form.
