# Mistake library — hero "Correction" interaction (P1.3)

Status: draft for owner review. Owner: Teacher Bek. Used by: HOME.HERO sentence, programme-page hero sentences (PROG.*.SENT), Level Ladder before/after, 404 device, the guide "The 10 mistakes Vietnamese learners make (and how we fix them)".

## How to read this file

- **Wrong (shown)** is the sentence typeset on screen. **Chars** counts every character including spaces and the final full stop. Hero rule: wrong sentence must be **28 characters or fewer** so it fits at display size on a 360 px phone in 2 lines.
- **Correction marks** use four primitives, so the SVG kit stays small:
  - **STRIKE** a single red line through a word or letters.
  - **CARET** a `^` with a short word or suffix written above the gap.
  - **REPLACE** strike plus the new word written above.
  - **REORDER** curved loop arrows swapping two words/phrases (add a caret when a word is also inserted).
- **Handwritten insert** is the text the owner's red pen has to write. Strike lines, carets, arrows and ✓ are shared marks.
- **VI note** is the one-line explanation for parents (shown after the correction lands, 1 line on desktop, 2 on mobile). The notes explain why a Vietnamese speaker makes the error (the mother tongue pattern), never "the child is wrong".
- Every corrected sentence was checked as natural, standard English (British and American both accept it). Where two corrections are possible, the one shown is the simplest.
- These sentences are **typical errors**, not claims about any real student. Do not caption them as a student's actual work unless the owner supplies real, consented samples. [OWNER: confirm you recognise these errors from your own classes; replace any that do not match what you hear (P2)]

## Notes for design and build

1. **Handwriting load (Q9).** The corrections above need these handwritten inserts: really, has, s, es, don't, is, do, on, am, went, ing, taller, a lot of homework, go, told, are, that I, ed, to, have lived, for (21 unique strings after sharing duplicates such as `s`, `is`, `ing`). Writing 21 words is more than the "about 10 words" the plan assumed. Options: (a) owner writes the full lowercase alphabet + apostrophe + space ×3 takes and the build composes words from glyphs; (b) owner writes only these 21 strings ×3 takes; (c) use fewer mistakes at launch (hero uses Band 1 only: 8 strings). [OWNER: choose (a), (b) or (c) (Q9)]
2. **Vietnamese text is never handwritten.** The owner's hand is limited to English words, numbers and symbols (✓ ✗ ○ ^ → ★ "Good!" "Try again" "Almost!" "tap me" signature). All Vietnamese explanations are typeset. This avoids an English-speaker handwriting stacked diacritics. [OWNER: agree (Q9)]
3. **Accessibility.** The text of both the wrong and corrected sentence must exist in the DOM; announce "Correction: `very` should be `really`" in an `aria-live="polite"` region; the corrected sentence is the final visible state under reduced motion.
4. **Tap-to-rotate.** Never repeat the same sentence twice in a row; keep a per-session list.
5. **Sensitivity.** Do not use a mistake whose wrong form is itself rude or could be misread; none here are.
6. **Verification notes.** `MIS.K08` "on foot" is the standard idiom. `MIS.T07` "a lot of homework" is correct in conversation; "much homework" is also accepted but less natural. `MIS.A03` "He suggested that I go" and "He suggested I go" are both correct. `MIS.A09`: "Despite the rain" or "In spite of the rain" is correct; "Despite of" is not. `MIS.T10`: "She told me she was late" is correct (the `that` is optional).
