## 3. Lighting and wardrobe

**Lighting**
- Use natural light from a window or open door. Keep the light on the face, not behind.
- Shoot in the morning or late afternoon. Avoid harsh midday sun.
- Use a white card or reflector to fill shadows.
- Set white balance by hand (custom, using a white card) so skin and paper look the same in every shot.
- Use the lowest ISO you can. Do not use flash.
- Keep the same lens family (35 mm or 50 mm) across all shots.
- Final warm grade (lifted blacks, slight cream highlight, light grain) will be applied later.

**Wardrobe**
- Teacher: plain, solid colours that do not compete with the red pen (for example navy, charcoal, cream, olive). No logos or text.
- Children and students: everyday clothes, no logos. Remove name tags.
- Bring a spare plain top for the teacher.

## 4. Technical specs

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Photo format | RAW + JPEG (highest quality, sRGB). Keep RAW. |
| Video resolution | 4K (3840 × 2160) for the master. Web versions will be 720p max, added later. |
| Video frame rate | 25 fps (matches 50 Hz mains power in Vietnam; avoids flicker under room lights) |
| Clip length | Record each clip at least 30 s so there is room to edit. Shot 9 at least 60 s. |
| Audio | External mic or lapel mic for shot 9. Test the sound before each take. |
| Memory | Two cards or two copies of the footage, copied at the end of each block |

---

## 5. What NOT to shoot

- Stock-style poses or posed "group photo" shots.
- Thumbs-up poses.
- Flags, Big Ben or other tourist clichés.
- Certificates photographed at an angle. (Flat, straight-on scans only, if used at all.)
- Blurred faces. (Use backs, hands or the room instead.)
- Anything that shows a child's name, school, home address or phone number.
- Any face of a child or parent without a signed consent form.

## 6. Delivery spec

**Folder structure** (one per shoot date)

```
2026-MM-DD_TeacherBek_shoot/
  01_RAW/            original RAW files, all shots
  02_JPEG/           JPEG exports, selects only
  03_VIDEO_4K/       master video files and clips
  04_NOTES/          shot checklist with ticks and any notes
```

Keep signed consent forms in a separate private folder. Do not copy them into the delivery folder.

**File naming**

- Photos: `TB_<shot#>_<short-description>_<take>.<ext>`
  - Example: `TB_01_portrait-whiteboard_01.jpg`
- Video: `TB_<shot#>_<short-description>_v<version>_4K.mp4`
  - Example: `TB_09_intro-60s_v01_4K.mp4`
- Use lowercase, hyphens, no spaces, no children's names in file names.
- Shot numbers match the table in section 1.

**Delivery:** Send the folder to the developer by the agreed link. Confirm every shot number has at least one file. Confirm each person with a face has a signed consent form on file.
