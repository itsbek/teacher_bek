# P1.6 — Handwriting sheet (20 marks × 3 takes)

Your red-pen marks become the site's signature. Write each mark **by hand, naturally**, as you would when correcting a student's work. Do not trace, do not practise on the sheet.

---

## Before you start

**Materials**
- Pen: red fine felt-tip, 0.5–0.7 mm (for example a Pilot or Stabilo fine red). Use the **same pen** for all 60 marks.
- Paper: plain white A4, no lines, no grid. 80 gsm or heavier so ink does not show through.
- Print: the 3 pages (Take 1, Take 2, Take 3) at **100% scale, no "fit to page"**. Print on plain white paper, no colour.
- Scale check: on page 1, the line in the header must measure exactly **50 mm**. If not, reprint at 100%.

**Test first**
- Draw a few strokes on scrap paper. The ink should look red, not orange or pink. If it looks off, change pens before starting.

---

## What to write (20 marks)

Box numbers are used in the file names and on the printed sheet.

| # | Mark / word | What to draw |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | ✓ | A tick, one stroke, the size of a large tick |
| 02 | ✗ | A cross, two strokes |
| 03 | Circle | One loop around an imaginary word, about 4 cm wide. Close the loop. |
| 04 | Caret ^ | A small inverted V, as if inserting a word above a line |
| 05 | Underline | One straight line, about 3 cm, under an imaginary word |
| 06 | Double underline | Two parallel straight lines under an imaginary word |
| 07 | Arrow → | Straight arrow, about 4 cm, with a clear head |
| 08 | Curved arrow | An arc with an arrowhead, like ↷ |
| 09 | Star | One star, about 2.5 cm across |
| 10 | "Good!" | The word "Good!" with the exclamation mark |
| 11 | "Try again" | The words "Try again" |
| 12 | "Well done" | The words "Well done" |
| 13 | "Nice!" | The word "Nice!" with the exclamation mark |
| 14 | "Almost!" | The word "Almost!" with the exclamation mark |
| 15 | ? | A question mark |
| 16 | ! | An exclamation mark |
| 17 | Your signature | Your usual signature, as you sign documents. Do not practise. |
| 18 | "Bek" | The word "Bek", normal capitals |
| 19 | Strike-through | One straight line through a short word of your choice (for example "very") |
| 20 | Margin bracket | A tall vertical bracket, about 4 cm high, like a curly "}" or a straight "]" |

**Sizes**
- Marks: fill about half to two-thirds of the box.
- Words: letter height about 1 cm. "Try again" and "Well done" may be a little smaller.
- Keep everything **inside its box**, with a small margin from the edge.

---

## How to write it

1. Start with **Take 1** (page 1). Work through boxes 01 to 20 in order.
2. Write each mark once, at natural speed. Do not repeat a mark because it looks wrong. Move on.
3. Do not look at Take 1 while writing Take 2. Do the same for Take 3.
4. If you make a mistake, do not erase it. Finish the page and write the box again on the next take.
5. Write the date in the header space of page 1 only.
6. Put the 3 pages in order (1, 2, 3) and keep them flat.

---

## Sheet layout (for the printer)

Each page is an A4 portrait grid. The same layout is printed on all three pages (Take 1, Take 2, Take 3).

- **Page margins:** 10 mm on all sides.
- **Header band:** top 20 mm, holds the title (for example "Take 1 of 3") and the 50 mm scale check line (page 1 only).
- **Grid:** 4 columns × 5 rows = 20 boxes per page.
- **Box size:** 46 mm wide × 49 mm high.
- **Gutter between boxes:** 2 mm.
- **Box outline:** thin light-grey line (0.25 pt). No fill.
- **Label:** small grey number and name in the top-left corner of each box (for example "03 Circle"). The label is light grey so it can be removed in the trace step. Do not write inside the label area.

Each mark sits in its own box, so the trace step can crop each one cleanly.

---

## Scanning

**Preferred: flatbed scanner**
- Resolution: **600 dpi**
- Colour: 24-bit colour (do not use greyscale or black-and-white; the red must be kept)
- Format: **PNG or TIFF** (lossless). Do not use JPEG.
- Turn off any auto-enhance, auto-crop or colour correction.
- Place the page flat, face down, aligned to the corner.

**Fallback: phone scan app**
- Use the "original" or "colour" mode. No filters, no auto-enhance, no black-and-white.
- Place the page on a flat table, in even daylight. Hold the phone parallel to the page.
- No shadows, no flash, no glare.
- Export as PNG. If the app cannot export at high resolution, tell the developer so they can decide whether to rescan.

**File names**
- `handwriting_take1.png`, `handwriting_take2.png`, `handwriting_take3.png`
- Use the same format for all three. Keep the original, unedited scans.

**Check before sending**
- All 20 boxes are visible on each page.
- No box is cut off at the edge.
- The red is clearly red.
- The scale check on page 1 reads 50 mm.

---

## Notes for the developer (P1.7)

- The vector set will use each box as a crop. Take 1, 2 and 3 are for choosing the best mark per box, not for combining.
- Do not clean up the marks by hand before sending the scans.
