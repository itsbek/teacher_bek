Questions people ask

1Where are the classes held?

My classroom is at Golden Mansion 1, 119 Phổ Quang, Phú Nhuận, Ho Chi Minh City. Students come from Phú Nhuận, Gò Vấp and Bình Thạnh.

2What ages do you teach?

Ages 5 to 17, in two groups: Young Learners (5–10) and Teens (11–17).

3How big are the classes?

Small groups, so every student speaks in every lesson.

4When are classes?

Twice a week for 90 minutes plus a 15-minute break. Weekday classes run 19:30–21:00; weekend classes run between 14:00 and 20:00.

5Can I try a class first?

Yes. The first week is free.

6Is there a contract?

No. After the free week, classes run month to month.

7What are your qualifications?

I hold TESOL and PGCE qualifications. I have taught for four years, about 2,500 students in total.

8What does "why-first" teaching mean?

I explain what a piece of language is for before asking anyone to remember it, then we use it in games and conversation. Read the approach.
